## Bottom line (updated after second research pass)

- **Clearest, highest-confidence risk (act on these first)**: atharvaveda_1, rigveda_1 (Sanskrit Sahitya Prakashan, 2015, ISBN) and mantra_maha_sagar (Chowkhamba Krishnadas Academy, 2017, ISBN, ©). All three are explicitly copyright-marked, currently-sold books from named active publishers — replace with a public-domain source or get permission.
- **Low risk (old, small/defunct publisher)**: valmiki_ramayana (1927) and mantra_shakti (~1974, Goyal & Company, Delhi).
- **Gita Press books (11 titles: agni_purana, garuda_purana_1, kathopanishad, nitya_karm_pooja, bhavishya_purana, narasimha_purana, bhagavad_gita_shankar, ishadi_upanishad, vishnu_purana_1, shiva_purana_1, shiva_purana_2)** are a shared medium risk — actively sold today at current reprint numbers, but also the Press's own low-cost dharmic literature. A single legal read on Gita Press's licensing stance would resolve this whole group at once rather than book-by-book. This is now 11 of 24 books — the single biggest risk cluster by count.
- **ekadashi_mahatmya**: identified as a Gaudiya Vedanta Publications (Srila Bhaktivedanta Narayana Maharaj lineage) release via its donor-credits page — active modern religious-publishing trust, treat as medium-high risk.
- **yajurveda**: one blurry footnote suggests a possible "Motilal Banarsidass, 1971" attribution — if true this would be a real, well-known academic Sanskrit publisher (i.e. a genuine risk, though an older one); not legible enough to confirm with certainty.
- **5 books remain genuinely unresolved even after checking both front AND back matter**: guru_granth_sahib (only a printer's name found, "Rohit Art Press, Delhi"), chanakya_neeti, nitya_devta_archana, rashi_muhurt_vigyan, samaveda. None of these show a publisher/copyright line anywhere in the scanned file. Closing these out fully would need either a physical copy in hand or a text-based web search for a matching edition — the scanned images themselves have been exhausted as a source.
