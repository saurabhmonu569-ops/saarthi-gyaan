### Jad — do, aur dono maanyatayein thi

**(a) Deprecation email aadha padha gaya.**


```
Roman      : ~3.5 akshar = 1 token
Devanagari : ~1 akshar   = 2 token     ← 7 guna mehnga
```
Isi ek baat se **do** kharabiyan aayi — `413` (input taraf) aur `MAX_TOKENS` (output taraf). Dono alag dikhti thi, jad ek thi.

### Teen pehre the, teenon nikal gaye

1. **Worker ka default** — anjaan model par request chup-chaap `llama-3.3-70b-versatile` par jaati thi. Jo suraksha-jaal tha, wahi har request ko mare hue model par bhej raha tha.

> **Teesri meri galti hai.** Release se pehle jo jaancha, wo wo hissa nahi tha jo badla tha.

### Sabse mehngi baat

Kharabi #3 — router Gemini ko sirf `429/5xx` par bulata tha. 23 July ko maan liya gaya tha ki *"404/413 client ki galti hai, dobara koshish bekaar."*

**Engine 2 poore samay taiyaar khada tha.** Gemini ki key lagi thi, `tryGemini()` chal raha tha, aur wo theek isi din ke liye banaya gaya tha.
